Welcome

Two places in one address: a hotel and a resort.

The Victorias Hotel sits right along Osmeña Avenue — the city's national highway — beside the Victorias City Coliseum. Step out the front door and you are on the main road; step out the back and you are at the Victorias Aquatic Center, known locally as Victorias City Resort.

The hotel opened on March 12, 2023, ahead of the 25th Kadalag-an Festival, as a city-led project to give Victorias a proper home for local and foreign visitors. Rooms are bright and practical, each with its own bathroom and modern plumbing and fixtures.

  • A city government hotel, opened in 2023
  • On the highway — easy to find, easy to reach
  • Resort pools at the back of the hotel

Three pools. One gate. Zero traffic.

Shallow water for the little ones, close enough for parents to watch from the shade.

Around Victorias

Worth the short drive.

Make Victorias your base — the city and its neighbours are an easy hop from the hotel.

St. Joseph, the Worker Chapel

The world-famous “Angry Christ” mural by Alfonso Vitug sits inside the chapel in the Victorias Milling Company (Vicmico) compound — one of Negros Occidental's most visited landmarks.

Gawahon Eco-Park & Falls

The city's favourite swim-and-picnic escape — about a 25 to 30-minute drive from the hotel.

City Coliseum & City Mall

The coliseum is next door — venue for sports and social events. City Mall is a 5-minute ride for dining and shopping, open 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
